/ setup — connect google sheet (expand for instructions)
  1. Create a new Google Sheet (any name).
  2. Go to Extensions → Apps Script. Paste this:
function doPost(e) {
  var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getActiveSheet();
  var data = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  sheet.appendRow([new Date().toLocaleString(), data.q1||'', data.q2||'', data.q3||'', data.q4||'']);
  return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({result:'success'})).setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
}
  1. Deploy → New deployment → Web app → Execute as: Me → Anyone → Deploy.
  2. Copy the Web app URL and replace https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycbxKSpPzM5EjF8GBjbLe5QfM6Z8X-Mcu4Jn2FJeXdxRRLXDkIANJArW8vRCMMxqpXdW8KA/exec in the script at the bottom of this file.

What do you say
to yourself when
work is too much?

Hi! I'm Aizat, an MA Interaction Design student. My diploma project is about the power of self-therapy. I believe that words have power — they can motivate or calm you. I'd love to hear yours!

What do you say to yourself in stressful moments when work or your obligations feel overwhelming? What calms you down when you're overthinking? What do you tell yourself when you feel like you haven't done enough, but actually it's time to stop working? What do you tell yourself when you're stressed or worried about work or study?

What to do?
1 Read the sentence starters below and use them to prompt you to recall a particular moment from your past. Imagine you are at work or studying and the workload is too much — for example, a graduation project.
2 Write down how you felt.
3 Hit submit. Your response is anonymous and will contribute to the development of a tool to help people in crisis.
