Hi! I'm Aizat, an MA Interaction Design student. My diploma project is about the power of self-therapy. I believe that words have power — they can motivate or calm you. I'd love to hear yours!

What do you say to yourself in stressful moments when work or your obligations feel overwhelming? What calms you down when you're overthinking? What do you tell yourself when you feel like you haven't done enough, but actually it's time to stop working?

What to do?
1Read the sentence starters below and recall a particular moment from your past when work felt overwhelming.
2Write down how you felt.
3Hit submit. Your response is anonymous and will contribute to the development of a tool to help people in crisis.
